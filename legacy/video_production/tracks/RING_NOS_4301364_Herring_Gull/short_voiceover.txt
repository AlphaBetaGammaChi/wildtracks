Scientists attached a tiny tracker to this Herring Gull. What it did next stunned biologists.

A relentless trek spanning 1 km across hostile terrain.

It began at Bjørvika, Oslo, Akershus & Oslo, Norway.

Burning up to half its body mass as fuel, it pushed relentlessly forward, crossing North Sea / Scandinavian Coastline.

Halfway through the journey at Operafjæra, Oslo, Akershus & Oslo, Norway, the tracker recorded extreme endurance under grueling conditions.

Finally, after covering 1 km, Gull JMT94 touched down at Operafjæra, Oslo, Akershus & Oslo, Norway.
