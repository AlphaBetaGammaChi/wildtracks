# White stork AR445 (short)
# Read each [scene] as written, leaving a short pause between scenes.
# To change the wording, edit scenes_<format>.json and run `wildtracks narration <track>`.

[s1]
This young white stork flew from Russia to South Africa. This is its real GPS track.

[s2]
In July 2013, researchers put a GPS tag on it in Russia. Its code: AR445.

[s3]
On 20 August 2013, AR445 set off south. In 78 days it flew at least 7,100 kilometres, through Ukraine, Bulgaria and 7 more countries, all the way to Ethiopia. Its route crossed the Sahara. Then it stopped for 29 days near Debre Markos in Ethiopia.

[s4]
On 3 December it moved on, heading south. In 51 days it flew at least 5,600 kilometres, through Kenya, Tanzania and 3 more countries, all the way to South Africa. Its route crossed the Kalahari Desert.

[s5]
On its very first migration, AR445 reached South Africa on 23 January 2014: at least 13,100 kilometres in 157 days.
