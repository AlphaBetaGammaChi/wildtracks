DER AR444 the white stork — a real GPS track

A wild white stork (Ciconia ciconia), tracked by GPS from
2013-08-19 to 2013-11-14: at least 9350 km, Russia → Ethiopia.
The whole recording spans 365.4 days.

Every position in this video is a real GPS fix. Distances are straight lines between
nightly roosts, so the true distance flown was higher.
